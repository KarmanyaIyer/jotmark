About

Jotmark keeps timestamped notes attached to the websites you visit. Notes can belong to a whole domain or to one page.

Everything is stored locally in your browser with chrome.storage.local. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. The extension never reads or changes the pages you visit; it only looks at the address of the tab you open it on.

Keyboard shortcut

The default shortcut to open Jotmark is Alt Shift J. Change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
